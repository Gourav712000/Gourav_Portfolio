About Me

I build the hiring and people systems that let startups scale without breaking. Over 6+ years, I've worked across the full spectrum of the people function — from hiring Computer Vision, Bare Metal, and Semiconductor engineers that most recruiters won't touch, to running the operations that keep a growing team functioning: payroll, HRMS, compliance, the full employee lifecycle, and grievance management. I grew with Devkraft from 5 people to 70, building its HRMS from scratch and owning everything from onboarding to exit along the way. I went on to specialize in deep-tech hiring at KoiReader and semiconductor recruiting at MCGBS, and I'm back at Devkraft today running recruitment, payroll, compliance, and HR operations end to end. My approach is systems-first: I use AI-assisted workflows to make hard hiring repeatable and HR operations transparent, not just reactive.
